# Scoreboard

Results are reported as a task-by-model matrix of raw outcomes, not as a headline percentage. On a set this small, a percentage would claim more precision than the data holds.

A cell records the model's outcome on the final prompt: **Fail** (the required outcome, in both browsing and closed-book modes), with the failure mode noted, or **Pass**, which sends the task back to redesign or to preseed rejection. Model name, version, and test date are recorded per run in the task's Step 5 file, because a difficulty claim expires as models improve.
